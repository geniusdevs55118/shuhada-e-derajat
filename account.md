## Quick Notes

- **Login URL:** `/login`
- You can enter just the **Username** (e.g. `helpdesk123`) or the full **Email** (e.g. `helpdesk123@demo.dsfhi.local`).
- You can also choose any role from the dropdown menu on the login page to automatically fill in both the username and password.
